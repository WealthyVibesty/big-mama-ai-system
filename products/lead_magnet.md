## Day 1 — Follow-Up That Never Forgets

**The problem:** Leads wait. You mean to reply, the day happens, and by the time you circle back the moment is gone.

**The workflow:**
1. New lead lands (form, DM, referral, missed call).
2. Confirmation message goes out in under two minutes, in your voice.
3. If no reply, a follow-up goes out on day 2 and day 5 automatically.
4. You only step in when a human conversation actually starts.

**Your content today:** Post the "manual work audit." List the three computer tasks that ate your yesterday. Owners recognize themselves in it instantly, and it opens the exact conversation this map finishes.

## Day 2 — The Content Calendar That Fills Itself

**The problem:** Content ships only when you find a spare hour. Spare hours do not exist.

**The workflow:**
1. Your voice profile and brand identity get written down once.
2. A weekly calendar is drafted from your offers and your audience's questions.
3. You approve in one sitting. The system posts on schedule.

**Your 5-day calendar (post these this week):**
- **Day 1:** The manual work audit (above).
- **Day 2:** "What I would automate first" — pick one workflow and walk through it.
- **Day 3:** A client moment. One real situation where follow-up saved or lost a deal. No names needed, no invented results.
- **Day 4:** The myth post. "AI tools are not the problem. Owning no system is."
- **Day 5:** The open door. Invite owners to get this map and see their own first workflow.

## Day 3 — Booking Without the Back-and-Forth

**The problem:** "What time works for you?" tennis burns days and loses calls.

**The workflow:**
1. One booking link, tied to your real availability.
2. Confirmation by text and email the moment someone books.
3. Reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before. No-shows get a same-day reschedule message.

**Your content today:** Post your booking link with one line: "This is what a second brain feels like on the other side."

## Day 4 — Outbound That Sounds Like You

**The problem:** Generic AI outreach reads like a robot wrote it, because one did, with no identity behind it.

**The workflow:**
1. Outbound is drafted from your voice profile, not a template pack.
2. Every message waits for your approval until you trust the pattern.
3. Replies land in one inbox, next to everything else.

**Your content today:** Share one sentence you actually say to customers, and why. Voice is the brand. This post proves yours exists.

## Day 5 — The Owner Dashboard

**The problem:** Everything lives in texts, emails, spreadsheets, and memory. You cannot improve what you cannot see.

**The workflow:**
1. Every lead, deal, project, and task in one command center.
2. Each morning it shows: follow-ups due, deals waiting, tasks overdue.
3. An AI assistant reads the same data and tells you the next action, not just the numbers.

**Your content today:** Post the question this whole map answers: "If your business ran for one week without you touching a computer, what would break first?" Then read the replies. That is your market research and your content plan in one thread.
